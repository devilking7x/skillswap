🔄 SkillSwap

Teach what you know. Learn what you don't. — सीखो, सिखाओ, आगे बढ़ो।

SkillSwap is a free skill-exchange board for students. Post one skill you can teach and one skill you want to learn. When someone teaches what you want to learn and wants to learn what you teach, that's a mutual match — a fair swap.

🔒 No accounts, no server, no tracking. Your posts live only in your browser (localStorage). The board ships with clearly labelled sample posts so you can see how it works — delete them any time with one click.

    How it works

    1. Post one skill you can teach and one you want to learn.
    2. Browse the board — filter by category, mode, or search.
    3. Match: a Mutual match means you can teach each other. A one-way result means they can teach you (you can still reach out).
    4. Connect using the contact detail on the post. SkillSwap never sees your messages.
